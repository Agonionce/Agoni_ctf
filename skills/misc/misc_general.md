## Knowledge

- Challenge text, artifact metadata, formats, and observed transformations are distinct evidence.
- Unknown categories benefit from reversible classification steps before specialization.
- Facts, hypotheses, and failed attempts should remain explicitly separated.

## Strategies

- Inventory provided evidence and identify the smallest unanswered classification question.
- Prefer reversible inspection and comparison before committing to a domain-specific theory.
- Re-route when new evidence creates a stronger Web, Pwn, Reverse, or Crypto signal.

## Heuristics

- Mixed file types or layered representations may require more than one domain skill.
- Repeated failure without new evidence is a signal to revisit classification assumptions.
